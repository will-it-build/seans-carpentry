How a job runs

Four steps, and you hear from us at each one

STEP 01

Measure

We come out, survey the space and talk through what you want it to do. No charge, and no obligation after.

STEP 02

Drawings & quote

You get a drawing, a timber schedule and a fixed price. Nothing gets cut until you have signed it off.

STEP 03

Machining

Timber is selected, machined and dry-assembled in the workshop, so site time stays short.

STEP 04

Fitting

We fit, finish and clear up after ourselves. Two years on workmanship, in writing.

Timber

Bought by the board, not by the pallet

Stock comes from two mills we have used for years. Everything is kiln-dried, checked for movement and left to settle in the shop before it is cut.
